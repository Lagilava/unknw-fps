The Eleven Minutes

Filed
Case ref 94-11
Duration
00:11:00 exact
Integrity
72% — tape stretch
Disposition
Closed, "equipment fault"

Nine clocks on nine separate circuits stopped inside the same second and restarted inside the same second, eleven minutes later. No shared power source. No shared fault path. The report does not explain the eleven minutes. It does not attempt to.

Equipment fault has a form. This has been filed as equipment fault four times.

It repeats. Same eleven minutes, same clocks, every tenth cycle since. No second report has ever been filed.

Verbatim — dispatch channel 2

02:47:13 "Confirm you're dark."

02:47:16 "Dark. Everything's dark."

02:58:13 "…back. Eleven on the nose. Who logged eleven?"

Works Record · Room Specification · Drawing set 6

The Room

Built
Predates current seal
Stated use
Archive, dry storage
Access
One door. No lock listed.
Integrity
Drawings complete

Walls exceed storage spec by a margin the drawings don't account for. No shelving in any revision. Door rated to three tonnes, hinged outward into the corridor — a swing that fails fire code and was approved anyway. No objection on file.

An outward swing keeps something in from being blocked on its way out. It does not keep anything out.

The seal has held on this door for every cycle on record but one.

Margin note, drawing 6.14 — unsigned

"Client insists on the outward swing. Told him it fails code. He said fire wasn't the concern."

Beyond the Seal

Two nights, on foot
Contact made
None
Standing order
Do not walk it alone
Integrity

Radios hold to the first junction and drop past it. Windows are lit on timers, not by anyone home — same cycle, every night, no variation. Cars are parked exactly as they were parked the first night. No rust. No movement.

Past the second junction the street repeats. Same corner, same three storefronts, however far you walk it.

The standing order exists because of night two, not night one. Nobody currently on this posting will discuss night two.

Last notebook page

"Walked two hours out. Ended back at the seal without turning around. Not filing this. Not going back out there alone."

Observation Series · Trials 1–9 · Room floor

Cloned Ghosts

Template
Operator, post-mortem
Print time
Under 40 seconds
Fidelity
Movement 1:1 · judgement degraded
Threat
High. Knows your habits.

Prints from a read taken while you were down on the floor, not from a live feed — it has been replaying that read since. Same angles you hold. Same corner you pre-fire. Same breaks for cover, half a second early, because it isn't deciding. It's repeating.

That's also the exploit. Nine trials, nine identical flanks: pressure it and it commits to the route you would have taken, which means you already know where it lands.

Trial 6 stopped in the doorway and didn't fire, for four seconds. Not repeated since. Not attempted since, either.
