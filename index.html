Operator Notes
Assault RifleForgiving at every distance. Lets you correct aim mid-burst, farm consistent XP, and hold doorways without committing. The right pick when wave composition is unknown.
Combat ShotgunDevastating inside 8 meters. A single trigger pull at point-blank ends most enemies before they can react. Positioning is everything — you die behind a bad reload if you let them reach mid-range.

Field Intelligence
Your Condition Clinically dead for an unknown interval. Motor function, memory, and weapon handling restored by the Echo at reactivation. Your body is a borrowed tool. The Echo will return it when the job is done — or it won't.
The Echo Non-human intelligence of unknown origin. It does not communicate in full sentences. It communicates in urgency. It revived you because the room requires a living witness with the clearance to fire — and because the dead remember what killed them.
The Wave Pattern Hostile contact follows a repeating escalation cycle. Each wave is harder, faster, and better informed about your position than the last. The room is feeding the enemy your signature. It has been doing this since the original breach.
Primary Objective
The Room Designation classified. Originally built as a sealed containment chamber — not to hold prisoners, but to hold information. Something inside it transmitted a signal that drew the first wave in. The door that opened wasn't broken. It was invited open. You are here to find out who sent that invitation.
Siege Drones Former escort and recovery units, re-tasked. They were designed to retrieve assets from hostile environments. Now the hostile environment is you. Engage at range. Their melee radius is wider than it looks and their lightning doesn't miss at contact distance.
Cloned Ghosts The chamber read your biometric signature and produced unauthorized copies. They move like you, shoot like a version of you that died afraid, and they know every angle in this room because they were printed from your own memory of it. Pressure them. Copies break under pressure.
The Pack Station Emergency weapons upgrade terminal, already active when you arrived. The Echo pre-authorized it. Spend XP, press E, upgrade. The room gets harder — your weapon ceiling rises with it. Do not let the enemy own the space around the station.
End Condition The breach closes when the pattern breaks. The pattern breaks when you clear every wave without dying. No extraction protocol exists. The Echo says you'll know what to do when the last one falls. It is not the most reassuring briefing you've ever received.
Last Transmission — Echo, timestamp unknown "The room kept your name in its teeth after you died. That means it isn't finished with you. Neither am I. Go back in. Clear the waves. When the signal stops, you'll understand what we were both trying to find."
